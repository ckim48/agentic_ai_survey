# Evidence boundary for the composite figure

## Supported claims

- The 30-seed trace-driven planner-surrogate evaluation reports AAI-CDOS
  deadline satisfaction of 99.17%, 97.28%, and 89.22% for 10, 25, and 50
  vehicles, respectively.
- At 50 vehicles, its P95 DT position error is 27.24 m.
- The sanitized real-GPT-4o audit contains structured O-RAN, CN, compute, and
  E2E-agent inputs/outputs, verifier feedback, execution, and memory updates.
- In audited event `n10_s2026_e1`, plan p0 is accepted at 139.2 ms under a
  1000-ms deadline and executes as ground access to ground1 compute.
- The 30-event audit records real returned token usage and API wall time.

## Unsupported or misleading claims to avoid

- Do not state that the aggregate 30-seed DT results were generated by GPT-4o.
- Do not include GPT-4o API wall time inside simulated network E2E latency.
- Do not claim that the real GPT-4o audit establishes better plan quality than
  One-Shot; AAI-CDOS and One-Shot selected the same plan in 30/30 audited events.
- Do not call the Seoul mobility feed measured radio/channel data.
- Do not portray the multi-round audited fallback event as an eventual
  deadline-feasible success; all three proposed plans were rejected and the
  final fallback missed the 350-ms deadline.
- Do not invent terminal IDs, vehicle coordinates, or raw trace timestamps for
  the infographic.
