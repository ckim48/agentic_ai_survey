\paragraph{Sensitivity analysis.}
We vary the reconciliation delay assigned to conflicting decisions of the
Independent Agents baseline from 0 to 120~ms. Even with no reconciliation
penalty, AAI-CDOS improves deadline satisfaction by 2.16, 9.61, and 13.31
percentage points for 10, 25, and 50 vehicles, respectively. Thus, the main
ordering does not depend on the default 80-ms conflict-delay assumption,
although its magnitude does. We also vary the maximum cross-domain negotiation
rounds from one to three. Moving from one to two rounds changes deadline
satisfaction by 0.00, 0.05, and 0.40 percentage points, respectively, while a
third round produces no additional completion gain in this configuration.
Accordingly, $R=3$ is retained as a safety cap rather than a source of reported
performance gain.
